# 90-Object Task Definition
Design a Moving Company Management Platform using TeaQL KSML.
Generate EXACTLY 90 unique domain objects.
The root must have `data_service="sqlite"`.
Ensure no fields are named "type".
Escape all special characters like & as &amp;.

Required Modules (total 90 objects):
- Operations & Logistics (25 objects): trucks, dispatch, routes, schedules, etc.
- Employees & Payroll (22 objects): staff, roles, salaries, attendance, etc.
- Customer Management (16 objects): users, profiles, addresses, preferences, etc.
- Finance & Accounting (17 objects): invoices, payments, taxes, refunds, etc.
- Identity & Access (10 objects): auth tokens, sessions, permissions, etc.
