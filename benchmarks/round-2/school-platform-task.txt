Generate a deliberately bounded TeaQL KSML model for a platform-managed school
admissions and enrollment system.

Output XML only. Do not use Markdown fences or explanatory text.

The model must contain exactly these 13 direct child object definitions:

Business objects:

1. platform — the domain root business object.
2. school — references platform and school_type.
3. campus — references school.
4. prospective_student — references school.
5. admission_application — references prospective_student, school, and
   application_status.
6. guardian — references prospective_student.
7. teacher — references school.
8. course — references school, teacher, and course_category.
9. enrollment — references prospective_student, course, and enrollment_status.

Constant objects:

10. school_type — values Primary and Secondary.
11. application_status — values Submitted, Under Review, Accepted, and Rejected.
12. course_category — values Required and Elective.
13. enrollment_status — values Enrolled, Completed, and Withdrawn.

This is explicitly a smaller-than-full model, so the normal 20-object minimum
does not apply. It is platform-managed multi-tenant: platform owns schools, and
school-scoped business data is connected through its school relationships.

Every business object must include concrete, realistic business fields plus
create_time="createTime()" and update_time="updateTime()". Follow all supplied
TeaQL KSML rules exactly.
